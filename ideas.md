## Idea 1: Industrial Brutalism + Organic Contrast

**Design Movement:** Neo-Brutalist with organic texture overlays

**Core Principles:**
- Raw, unapologetic typography — heavy black weight on stark backgrounds
- Organic textures (grass, soil, bark) bleeding into hard geometric containers
- Asymmetric column splits: 60/40 or 70/30 layouts, never centered grids
- Neon lime green (#B5E61D) as the single accent — used sparingly but boldly

**Color Philosophy:**
- Near-black (#0D0D0D) as the dominant base — conveys professionalism and strength
- Off-white (#F0EDE6) for text and card surfaces — warm, not clinical
- Neon lime green (#B5E61D) for CTAs, tags, and highlights — energy of fresh-cut grass
- Earthy brown (#3B2A1A) for subtle section dividers and texture overlays

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed sections with hard horizontal cuts
- Left-anchored headlines with right-side imagery bleeding off-canvas
- Service cards in a staggered brick-like grid, not a uniform row

**Signature Elements:**
- Thick 3–4px neon-green rule lines used as section separators
- Uppercase condensed headings with tracked letter-spacing
- Diagonal slash dividers between sections

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states: neon green border slides in from left on cards
- CTA buttons: fill sweeps from left on hover
- Scroll-triggered fade-up for content blocks

**Animation:**
- Entrance: staggered fade-up with 0.1s delay per element
- Hover: 200ms ease-out border/fill transitions
- No decorative looping animations — purposeful only

**Typography System:**
- Headings: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold (900) — compressed, powerful
- Body: DM Sans Regular — clean and modern
- Labels/Tags: Barlow Condensed SemiBold, all-caps, tracked

## Idea 2: Utility-Craft — Field Manual Aesthetic

**Design Movement:** Military/Utility Field Manual meets modern service brand

**Core Principles:**
- Everything looks like it was designed to work, not to impress
- Grid lines, measurement marks, and functional labels as decorative elements
- Warm olive/khaki palette with a single electric accent
- Dense information hierarchy — lots of content, clearly organized

**Color Philosophy:**
- Warm khaki (#C8B89A) as background — like weathered paper
- Deep forest green (#1A3A2A) for text and structure
- Electric yellow (#FFD700) for accent — like a warning label
- Dark charcoal (#1C1C1C) for high-contrast sections

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Newspaper-style multi-column text blocks
- Service cards styled like field manual entries with numbered labels
- Booking form styled like a work order form

**Signature Elements:**
- Dashed border boxes around key content
- Numbered section labels (01, 02, 03)
- Monospace font for data/specs

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Minimal animation — functional hover states only
- Form fields with clipboard/notepad aesthetic

**Animation:**
- Subtle fade-in on scroll
- No decorative motion

**Typography System:**
- Headings: Space Grotesk Bold
- Body: IBM Plex Sans
- Labels: IBM Plex Mono

## Idea 3: Bold Terrain — Dark Athletic Brand

**Design Movement:** Athletic/Sports Brand meets Outdoor Services

**Core Principles:**
- Dark, dramatic backgrounds with high-contrast neon green accents
- Photography-forward — real yard work photos as hero elements
- Bold diagonal geometry and slash motifs throughout
- Every section feels like a product launch, not a service listing

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep charcoal (#111111) as base — premium, serious
- Neon lime (#7FFF00 / chartreuse) as primary accent — alive, energetic
- Pure white for body text — maximum contrast
- Dark green (#0A2A0A) for subtle section tinting

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-viewport hero with diagonal image crop
- Services in a horizontal scroll on mobile, 3-column on desktop
- Booking section split: left = bold headline + contact, right = form panel

**Signature Elements:**
- Diagonal slash motifs cutting across section boundaries
- Neon green pill/badge labels on service cards
- Large background text (e.g., "YARD" "CLEAN" "MULCH") as decorative layer

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Cards lift with shadow on hover
- Navigation links underline slides in from left
- Form fields glow green on focus

**Animation:**
- Hero text: word-by-word slide-up entrance
- Cards: staggered fade-up on scroll
- Buttons: scale 1.02 + glow on hover

**Typography System:**
- Headings: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold — all caps, massive scale
- Body: Inter Medium — clean, readable
- Accent labels: Barlow Condensed SemiBold, tracked

## Selected Approach: **Idea 1 — Industrial Brutalism + Organic Contrast**

Heavy Barlow Condensed headings on near-black backgrounds, neon lime green (#B5E61D) as the single accent, asymmetric layouts with left-anchored content, and staggered scroll animations. This feels crafted and distinctive — not like a generic service website template.
