## Design
- **Color Scheme:** Black background, bright neon green (#00FF00 or similar) accents, white text
- **Style:** Bold, high-contrast, uppercase typography, modern/aggressive outdoor brand aesthetic
- **Font:** All-caps heavy sans-serif headings

## Sections
1. **Hero Section** – "Restore Your Outdoor Appearance" headline, "Now Booking" badge, phone/email contact, CTA buttons (Book Your Service / View Services), feature tags (Maintenance, Cleanup, Mulch, Powerwash), description text, photo of yard/walkway, feature list (Clean Lines, Sharp Finishes, Easy Scheduling)
2. **Services Section** – "Practical Outdoor Work With A Strong Finished Look"
   - Yard Maintenance (mowing, edging, trimming, seasonal upkeep)
   - Cleanups & Removals (leaf cleanup, debris clearing, overgrowth removal)
   - Mulch, Garden Beds & Washing (mulch installation, planting support, power washing)
3. **Before/After Project Gallery** – Shows before/after images of yard work, exterior detail proof
4. **Booking Section** – "Choose Your Service and Request the Appointment"
   - Service checkboxes: Yard Maintenance, Yard Cleaning, Lay Mulch, Gardening (Planting/Garden Bed), Powerwash
   - Form fields: Full Name, Phone Number, Email, Service Address, Preferred Day, Preferred Time, Project Notes
   - Live Booking Summary
   - CTA: "Request Booking" button, "Email Instead" link
5. **Footer** – Business name, tagline, phone, email

## Functionality
- Service selection checkboxes with live booking summary
- Booking form (mailto or similar submission)
- Responsive layout
